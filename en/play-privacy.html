Inlyra Mail / Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Summary
Inlyra Mail does not collect mail into a developer-operated server. Mail data is handled between the device and the mail provider selected by the user.

1. Operator and scope

This policy applies to Inlyra Mail Version 1.3, an Android app developed independently by ふぁん.

2. Data and purposes

The app handles email addresses, IMAP/SMTP settings, credentials, message headers and bodies, attachments, folders, drafts, recipient suggestions, and notification, theme, sync, and app-lock settings as needed to send, receive, display, search, notify, secure, and retry mail operations. The app does not use advertising, behavioral analytics, profiling, or sale of personal data.

3. Storage and deletion

Mail data, settings, attachments, and sender-icon caches are stored in the Android app or cache area. Removing an account, clearing app data, or uninstalling removes related local data. Mail and accounts on the provider are not deleted.

4. Connections

IMAP/SMTP connections go directly to the mail provider configured by the user. Sender icons and external HTML images allowed by the user may connect directly from the device to their providers. The developer does not operate mail storage or an image proxy.

5. Security

Credentials are encrypted on the device using a key managed by Android Keystore. JavaScript and external images in HTML mail are restricted by default. The app does not collect or store biometric data.

6. Disclosure and third-party terms

Except where required by law, the developer does not sell or provide message content, attachments, or credentials to third parties. Android, Google Play, mail providers, and external browsers apply their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Contact and changes

Contact information is provided on the Google Play store listing. This policy may be updated when the app, external services, or applicable law changes.
