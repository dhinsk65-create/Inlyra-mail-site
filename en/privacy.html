Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

In brief
Inlyra Mail does not collect your mail into a developer-operated server. Mail connections go directly from the app to the mail providers you configure, and mail data and credentials are normally handled on your device.

1. Operator and scope

This policy is provided by ふぁん, an independent developer, for the Android app “Inlyra Mail” and its later versions.

2. Information handled by the app

2.1 Accounts and credentials

The app handles the email address, IMAP/SMTP host, port, security mode, sync mode, and the password or Gmail app password entered by you. The developer does not receive your normal Gmail password.

2.2 Mail and attachments

To display and operate mail, the app handles headers such as sender, recipients, subject, date and Message-ID, as well as message bodies, attachments, folders, drafts, outbox items and mail flags.

2.3 Contacts and recipient suggestions

The app stores contacts you create and uses recent senders from locally cached headers to suggest recipients (up to 300 recent sender entries). It does not request Android’s broad contacts permission or read your phone address book.

2.4 Settings and diagnostics

The app stores theme, notification, notification-preview, per-account sync, folder-order, blocked-sender, trusted-image-sender and biometric app-lock settings. Local sync diagnostics contain an account identifier, operation type, outcome, error category, duration and timestamp, but not message bodies or passwords.

3. Purposes

These data are used only for sending and receiving mail, displaying and searching mail, applying server-side read/star/folder actions, notifications, drafts and attachments, recipient suggestions, retrying sync, and improving stability and security. The app is not designed for advertising, behavioural profiling or selling data.

4. Storage and retention

Mail data, drafts, attachments, contacts, settings and sender-icon caches are stored in Android app-private or cache storage. Automatic app backup is disabled. Data remains until you remove an account, clear app data or uninstall the app; caches may expire earlier due to size or age limits.

5. Connections and third parties

  • Mail providers: IMAP/SMTP connections go directly to the Gmail or mail provider configured by you. The provider receives the data required to authenticate, synchronize and send mail.
  • Sender icons: The app may request favicon files over HTTPS from the sender’s domain. That domain may receive your IP address and normal request metadata.
  • External HTML images: External images are restricted by default. If you choose to show them or trust a sender, the image host may receive your IP address, time and User-Agent. The request is direct and does not pass through a developer proxy.
  • Links: Links you tap open in Android or another browser, where the destination’s policy applies.
  • Ko-fi and payment providers: When you open the optional tip page, Ko-fi and its payment providers handle the payment in an external browser. The app does not collect or store card details.

6. Security

Account credentials are encrypted on the device with AES-GCM using a key managed by Android Keystore. Mail transport follows the security mode you select. SSL/TLS or STARTTLS is recommended; choosing no encryption may expose mail to network observers. HTML JavaScript and risky elements are restricted by default. Android handles biometric authentication; the app does not receive or store biometric data.

7. Notifications

If enabled, the app can show new-mail, send-result and snooze notifications. With notification previews enabled, sender and subject may appear. Android lock-screen settings also apply, and notifications can be disabled both in the app and in Android settings.

8. Sharing and analytics

Except where required by law, the developer does not sell or provide mail, attachments, credentials or contacts to third parties. The app includes no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK and no developer-operated mail-storage server. Android, app distribution services, mail providers and external browsers apply their own terms.

9. Deleting an account and local data

Use the account-delete action in the app’s Settings to remove that account’s connection settings, credentials, locally cached headers/bodies/attachments, drafts, outbox items, sync diagnostics, pins, subscription data and related caches. Background sync and notifications are stopped. Contacts are independent of an account and can be deleted separately. Mail on the provider’s server is not deleted; use the provider’s own tools for that.

10. Children

Inlyra Mail is not directed primarily to children. Do not enter personal information or mail credentials without appropriate parental or guardian permission.

11. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app, external services or applicable law changes. The update date and material changes will be reflected on this page. For questions, use the Support page or the Ko-fi page. Do not include passwords, full message content or other sensitive information in a support request.
