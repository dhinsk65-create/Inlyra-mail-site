Inlyra Mail / Android

Google Play information

Inlyra Mail is an independently developed email app for Gmail and standard IMAP/SMTP accounts.

Key features

  • Up to five email accounts
  • Unified inbox, folders, drafts, sent mail, and trash
  • Local message cache, search, attachments, and notifications
  • Controls for external images and JavaScript in HTML mail
  • Biometric or device authentication app lock

Use and access

  • The app is free to use
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs are included
  • Gmail accounts currently use app passwords
  • OAuth is not required by the current public build

Privacy and safety

Mail content, attachments, account settings, and credentials are handled between the device and the mail provider selected by the user as required for mail features. The developer does not operate a hosted mail storage service.

External images and JavaScript are restricted by default. Removing an account removes its related local data from the device.
